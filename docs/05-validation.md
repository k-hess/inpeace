# What's unproven, and how we find out

As of August 17, 2026, after the second group call. The research is done for now; what's left is validation, and the group can run most of it better than we can.

## What we have

- The prototype: three doors, Texas and California rules engine with computed dates, the inventory, guidance cards, care circle, ripple invitation, mock handoff. Demo scenarios ready.
- Hard demand data from two keyword pulls ([01-search-demand.md](01-search-demand.md)), with budget left for more.
- A read on the companies that came before us ([02-prior-companies.md](02-prior-companies.md)).
- The group: reach, press, and advertising skills, first-hand loss experience, and networks full of it.
- Kelly's Comfort Club and her Comfort Food podcast as a live community and channel.

## What's unproven

1. Whether the group's reach converts to this audience. Never inventoried. This is the last untested thesis-killer.
2. Whether anyone uses the executor product. Zero real users so far.
3. Whether they'll pay, and in what shape. $99 to $249 self-serve looked like a ceiling in the research; high-ticket guided looked better. Untested with humans.
4. Whether the ripple invitation converts.
5. Whether the repatriation edge-case cards are accurate against primary sources.

## What the second call said (August 17)

Kyle walked the group through the deck and the prototype. No pushback on direction: free before-death tier, revenue at the settlement stage, no funeral-home ranking, partner and referral revenue over charging the person grieving. Feedback was about the experience, not the plan:

- The plan page reads as a lot on a phone. People want to be walked through it a card at a time, not scrolled through a long page.
- Back navigation in the intake restarted from the beginning. Fixed.
- The faith step moved on after one tap; people wanted to pick more than one. Fixed.
- Inventory hints were too dense. Now collapsed until tapped.
- The vault's security model needs real work before it holds anything sensitive.
- New asks: notary integration, a frozen-accounts explainer, remains transport as a guided or concierge flow, task splitting for estranged family, obituary logistics, expense tracking during settlement.

Kelly's questions from her slides (obituary placement, how family learns the plan exists, dying away from home, bank accounts by name and last four instead of logins, multiple properties, friend-funded funeral costs, multiple vehicles, employer life insurance) are being added as content. The one that isn't a content edit is how the family finds out the plan exists, which is the handoff trigger, still unsolved.
