# In Peace: research and plan

The thinking behind the product and the group deck (`public/deck.html`, live at inpeace.kylehess.workers.dev/deck.html). Read in order; each one is short.

1. [What the search data says](01-search-demand.md). Two keyword pulls, 1,100 terms. Where the volume is, where the paying intent is, and why nobody searches for the product category.
2. [The companies that came before us](02-prior-companies.md). Who tried this, who charged for what, and how each ended.
3. [The kickoff call](03-kickoff-call.md). What the group landed on August 4, where the research pushed back, and what got built next.
4. [The phased plan](04-roadmap.md). What we're saying no to, the bet underneath, and phases 1 to 3.
5. [What's unproven](05-validation.md). The open questions and who's running down each one.

`data/` has the raw keyword CSVs and the scripts that produced them.
